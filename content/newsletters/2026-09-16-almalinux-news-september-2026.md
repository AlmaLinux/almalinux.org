## New on the AlmaLinux Blog

**Meet the 2026 Board Election Candidates**

Voting for the 2026 board election opened on September 14 and closes on October 2! If you were a member in good standing by July 16, your ballot link will be sent to the email address associated with your membership in [accounts.almalinux.org](https://accounts.almalinux.org). Though we have two open seats and two nominees, your votes still matter because we still need 50% of the foundation members to vote.

[Learn more in this blog post](/blog/2026-09-14-2026-election-candidates/).

**AlmaLinux 10.3 Beta Now Available!**

We're proud to announce the availability of AlmaLinux 10.3 Beta "Mauve Lion" for all supported architectures! AlmaLinux 10.3 Beta introduces new compiler toolsets, new language runtimes, and improved security. This is also the first Enterprise Linux 10 release to carry the mod_security web application firewall and the OWASP Core Rule Set. Finally, Podman 6 moves container configuration out of /etc.

A usual reminder: this is a BETA release. It should not be used for production installations.

[Get the full release notes in this blog post](/blog/2026-09-10-announcing-103-beta/).
