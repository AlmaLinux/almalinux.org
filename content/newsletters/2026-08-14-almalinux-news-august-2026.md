## Community Spotlight

**The Media & Entertainment SIG**

If you haven't heard of the AlmaLinux Media & Entertainment SIG, it is a Special Interest Group dedicated to supporting and enhancing the use of AlmaLinux within the professional visual effects, animation, and post-production industries.

One of the amazing things they've put together is [the AlmaLinux Creative Installer](https://kernelchief.github.io/almalinux-creative-installer/), a one-click solution for independent creatives on AlmaLinux that allows users to install Blender, DaVinci Resolve, GIMP, Krita and 40+ more apps without an IT team.

If this is of interest to you and you want to get involved, they're always looking for more members! They hold monthly meetings on the first Wednesday of each month at 16:00 UTC. You can also [learn more here](https://wiki.almalinux.org/sigs/MediaAndEntertainmentSIG.html#how-to-join).

**SHIM update! Microsoft 2011 Secure Boot certificates have expired, and AlmaLinux users are covered!**

The Microsoft UEFI Secure Boot certificates from 2011 have expired. The short version for AlmaLinux users: everything is covered, and your systems will not stop booting. There is nothing you have to do right now, but a quick check will make sure you are ready for future shim updates.

[Read about what happened and what we recommend in this blog post](/blog/2026-07-14-secure-boot-2023-certificates/).

**GhostLock (CVE-2026-43499) kernel privilege escalation: Patch released**

GhostLock (CVE-2026-43499) is a use-after-free in the kernel's real-time mutex (rtmutex) priority-inheritance code. The patched kernel for GhostLock has been released to the production repositories for every affected AlmaLinux release: 8, 9, and 10.

[Learn more in this blog post](/blog/2026-07-09-ghostlock/).
