**AlmaLinux Vagrant boxes have a new home: vagrant.almalinux.org**

HashiCorp is retiring the HCP Vagrant registry (formerly Vagrant Cloud), the service that has hosted the almalinux/\* Vagrant boxes since 2021. To keep the boxes available, AlmaLinux now runs its own Vagrant box catalog at vagrant.almalinux.org. It has every version that was ever published to HCP, plus all new releases.

[Read more in this blog post](/blog/2026-11-02-vagrant-catalog/).

**Help us bring almalinux.org to more languages**

We have an amazing community of translators who contribute translations for the AlmaLinux website. However, that does not mean that the work is complete. If you would like to help translate the website into new languages, it's actually very easy to get started!

[Learn more about how you can help in this blog post](/blog/2026-10-28-help-us-translate-almalinux-org/).

**DISA STIG automation for AlmaLinux OS 9**

TuxCare contributed free DISA STIG automation for AlmaLinux OS 9 to the ComplianceAsCode project! That means you can now point OpenSCAP at an AlmaLinux OS 9 system and get a report of which STIG requirements it meets, rather than working through a few hundred of them by hand.

[Read more in this blog post](/blog/2026-10-26-disa-stig-automation-almalinux-os-9/).

**The Marketing SIG is now the Promo SIG**

We've officially renamed the Marketing SIG the Promo SIG! Nothing about what the Promo SIG does will be changing, but we've made the decision to change the name to better reflect what the SIG actually does.

[Learn more by reading this blog post](/blog/2026-10-16-the-marketing-sig-is-now-the-promo-sig/).
