<h2 id="booth">Come Find Us at Booth #79</h2>

We're back in Raleigh for All Things Open, and we'd love to see you. Stop by booth #79 (right next to the Keynote room) to meet people who work on AlmaLinux regularly, tell us what you're running, and ask us anything.

<h2 id="community-certification">Certify From Anywhere</h2>

You don't have to be in Raleigh to help. Community validation is a real part of how the catalog works: when a community member certifies a machine, they run the same suite that we and the hardware vendors run, and every result adds to the confidence in a listing. The more of us that prove something works, the better!
