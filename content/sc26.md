<h2 id="booth">Come find us at booth #1704</h2>

We're heading to Chicago for SC26, and we'd love to see you. Stop by booth #1704 to meet people who work on AlmaLinux, tell us what you're running on your clusters, and ask us anything. <h2 id="community-certification">Certify from anywhere</h2>

You don't have to be in Chicago to help. Community validation is a real part of how the catalog works: when a community member certifies a machine, they run the same suite that we and the hardware vendors run, and every result adds to the confidence in a listing. The more of us that prove something works, the better!

      <h3>Publishing the Repository Is the Easy Part: Taking Research Software Public</h3>
      <p>Research groups across the HPC (high performance computing) industry build universally useful software all the time, but most of it is never shared with the world. Open sourcing it can fix that, but publishing a project is much more than publishing the repository.</p>
      <p>benny walks through the decision the way she walks through it with the many teams who ask her: finding your real motivation, whether your institution is on board, what long-term support you can honestly commit to, and what structure fits (an informal group, an umbrella foundation, fiscal sponsorship, or your own). Then she covers the practical side: getting the code ready, getting the project ready to receive feedback, getting yourself ready for contributors, and defining what success looks like before you need it. She'll post the slides and a checklist here afterward.</p>
